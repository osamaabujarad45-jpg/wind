# Corporate claims evidence matrix

_As of 2026-11-13. Verdicts: supported, partly supported, unsupported, contradicted, not independently verifiable._

## Claims matrix

| Claim | Verdict | Evidence / correction |
|---|---|---|
| Uber completed its acquisition of Delivery Hero. | Unsupported | Only 66.56% of Delivery Hero shares and votes were attributable to Uber's bidder after the first acceptance period (Eurex, 2026-11-10). Settlement was still subject to further offer conditions; closing was expected in H2 2027, not completed as of this evidence. |
| e& completed the sale of 12.5% of its 50.03% stake in Careem Technologies to Uber. | Supported | e&'s completion announcement is dated 31 July 2026 and states "the successful completion of the sale" for a total cash consideration of USD 100 million. |
| e& retains 37.53% of Careem Technologies after the sale. | Supported | e&'s June 2026 divestment announcement explicitly states: "Upon completion of the transaction, e& will retain 37.53% ownership in the Company." The July completion filing confirms the sale completed but does not restate the retained percentage. |
| Uber owns 62.47% of Careem Technologies after the sale. | Partly supported | 62.47% is the arithmetic complement of e&'s stated 37.53% retained stake, and is reported by secondary sources. It is derived, not stated in the e& completion filing itself. |
| Uber had 50 million Uber One members generating approximately half of Mobility and Delivery Gross Bookings. | Supported | Uber's Q1 2026 results report "reaching 50 million Uber One members" and members "driving half of our Gross Bookings across Mobility and Delivery" — not a verified ">50%" claim. |
| HAAT raised $200m in its latest funding round. | Contradicted | HAAT raised approximately $20m (NIS 60m) at a post-money valuation of roughly $100m (NIS 310m) in April 2026 (Calcalist, 2026-04-05). |
| HAAT's valuation is $300m. | Not independently verifiable | The $300m figure is attributed to a company PR representative (CNN, 2026-09-20). The April 2026 round's $100m post-money valuation is the stronger transaction-based benchmark. |
| HAAT was founded in 2022. | Contradicted | Start-Up Nation Finder dates founding to January 2019; Tracxn and Infobip say 2020. Calcalist (April 2026) describes the round as the fourth "since its founding five years ago," consistent with ~2021. No source supports 2022. |
| HAAT operates in 50 cities with 10,000+ restaurant/market partners. | Supported | CNN (2026-09-20) reports 50 cities and more than 10,000 restaurants and markets; HAAT's own newsroom corroborates "thousands of restaurants across 50 cities." HAAT's homepage states >40 cities and 10,000+ partners (likely a less-updated figure). These are company-supplied metrics, not independently audited. |
| HungerStation holds approximately 40% of the Saudi food-delivery market. | Not independently verifiable | The 40% figure is a Momentum Works estimate as of end-2025, carried by secondary reporting (PYMNTS, 2026-09-24). It is not an audited company figure. HungerStation's own reported metrics: 102 cities, 65,000 partners, 61% of Saudi GMV via subscription. |
| HungerStation is a Delivery Hero brand acquired by Uber. | Partly supported | HungerStation is listed among Delivery Hero businesses being acquired by Uber in the July 2026 offer. However, the acquisition has not closed — it remains subject to the same conditions as the broader Delivery Hero offer. |
| Careem launched ride-hailing services in Gaza and Nablus, Palestine. | Supported | Arab News (2018-01-12) and MENAbytes (2018-01-08) report the launches. Service was announced in January 2018. This is historical evidence; current 2026 operational status is not confirmed. |
| Uber One has 50m+ members generating >50% of Gross Bookings. | Partly supported | Uber's Q1 2026 release supports "reaching 50 million" members, but says they drive "half" of Gross Bookings across Mobility and Delivery; ">50%" overstates the disclosed figure. |
| Uber's trailing-12-month free cash flow is approximately $10.4 billion. | Supported | Q3 2026 results report quarterly FCF of $2.535bn and nine-month FCF of $7.613bn. The ~$10.4bn TTM figure is consistent with adding Q4 2025 FCF of $2.8bn to nine-month 2026 FCF of $7.613bn. |

## Provenance notes

- **Primary (filings/IR):** e& corporate announcements, Uber IR press releases, Eurex corporate-action notices. These are regulatory or company filings with legal disclosure obligations.
- **Secondary (reported):** CNN, Calcalist, Arab News, MENAbytes, PYMNTS. Reliable journalism but may carry company-supplied figures.
- **Company-reported marketing:** HAAT website metrics, HungerStation city/partner counts. Useful for scale but not independently audited.
- **Derived:** Uber's 62.47% Careem stake is the complement of e&'s stated 37.53%. The $10.4bn TTM FCF is the sum of disclosed quarterly figures.
- **Private valuations:** HAAT's $100m post-money is transaction-based (Calcalist). The $300m figure (CNN, attributed to PR) is not independently verifiable.
- **Market-share estimates:** HungerStation's 40% is a Momentum Works estimate, not an audited figure.
