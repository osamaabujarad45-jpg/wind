## 2. Design plan (frontend-design pass, corrected)

- Colour: paper `#F3F2EC`, forest `#12342A` (ink and primary action), olive `#34765A` (pickup and confirmation), clay `#CB6845` (destination and markers, text only as `clayInk` `#A94A28`), lime `#D3F27A` (on forest only). Unchanged from `palette`; this pass added no colour.
- Type: the platform system face through `Txt` (h1 21, h2 17, title 15, body 14, caption 12, micro 11). No new font; weight carries hierarchy.
- Layout: one column, reading-direction alignment from `useCopy().align`, the route spine (olive dot to clay square) as the signature on trips, tracking and jobs. Cards group related facts; settings use list rows.
- Principles: one job per written element; the route spine is the memorable element; no hero, no gradient, no decorative eyebrow; every CTA names its outcome.
- Review against the brief: the generic defaults this screen set avoided were (a) a decorative hero on the home screen (not built), (b) a promotional section heading over the home promo rail (removed in this pass; the two cards name themselves, and `docs/PRODUCT.md` asks for a calm home rather than a promotional front), and (c) a receipt "thank you" line (removed in this pass). The "Services" heading on home is kept deliberately: it labels the three core actions and gives the screen a heading landmark on the default state (the active-order and recent sections only render when they have content), so removing it would cost screen-reader navigation rather than save a decoration.
