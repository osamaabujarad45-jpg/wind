# Map-first app: what changed on 4 November 2026

The owner reported that the maps could not be used for real day-to-day work and the backend was not stable, with Uber as the reference and Tulkarm as the first city to get right.

## What was actually wrong

1. **Fixes never reached users.** Production ran the 31 October image while PRs #4, #5 and #6 sat merged or open, and the installed APK was 1.0.13 from the same day.
2. **The app was a long form with a small map.** Booking meant choosing a city from a list, typing landmarks by hand and reading a 150 px map. Tracking was a 300 px map on a long page. Drivers saw a card list.
3. **Search could not serve one town.** It worked on an explicit tap through public Nominatim, at one request a second.
4. **Regions were missing.** Gaza, Khan Younis, Rafah, Deir al-Balah, Tubas and Jerusalem were outside the service map.
5. **One failed request could blank the app**, with no automatic retry.
6. **Go online could only fail in production.** Driver presence needs PostgreSQL, and production runs SQLite. Found on the phone; drivers there now get the jobs board directly.
7. **Three navigation and keyboard bugs only a real trip showed.** The keyboard covered a closed sheet, so a driver typed the handover code blind. The turn banner picked the nearest maneuver in a straight line, so a route looping away from a stop 100 m off said "arrive". A navigating driver's route started from a 110 m grid cell, which can be the next street.

## What it is now

| Section | Change |
| --- | --- |
| Map engine | `public/map.html` is the one map on every platform (WebView on phones, same-origin iframe on web). Calm base style that keeps landmarks, the customer's own dot with accuracy, car marker, cased route line, labelled stops with minutes, camera and pin that stay clear of the app's chrome, side sheet on sideways phones. |
| Places | `server/domain/place-index.ts`: 24,090 places and 2,637 streets from OpenStreetMap, Arabic-aware, answered in milliseconds per keystroke. 843 places within 6 km of central Tulkarm. Reverse lookup names any pin. Nominatim stays as the wider fallback. |
| Booking | Map from the first screen. GPS is the pickup; type a destination; see road, minutes and price; one tap requests. Details open on demand, and only what is missing is asked for. Recent and saved places carry their pins from past orders. |
| Tracking | Full-screen map with status, minutes, driver and call on a sheet. The camera frames the car with the next stop. |
| Driver | Home is on the map with offers on the sheet, naming neighbourhoods and the rough distance to pickup. On a job: turn-by-turn banner from OSRM maneuvers, measured along the road and in the app's language, and a follow camera. The route starts at the driver's own fix and refreshes on 250 m of movement or on leaving the line. GPS stays off until online, on a job, or the driver's own Locate tap for that visit. |
| Stability | Failed reads retry once; a failed refresh no longer replaces a working screen; the offline screen retries by itself. On the VM: nightly verified SQLite backup to Azure Blob plus 14 days on disk, a watchdog for the API and router, and a deploy script that rolls back on failure. |

## Not done, plainly

- **Not Uber parity.** Traffic-aware ETAs, voice guidance, venue entrances, payment rails and staffed operations are absent. OSRM has no live traffic, so minutes are estimates.
- Not tested on iOS hardware. Battery and long-session memory were not measured.
- Fares are the existing tariff (₪23 for 2.6 km in Tulkarm); the owner may want a city rate.
- Production has no admin or approved driver yet. Those need the owner's own registration first.
