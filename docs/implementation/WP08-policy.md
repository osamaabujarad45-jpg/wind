# WP08 routing policy — preparatory slice

This isolated, inactive pure function classifies supplied evidence; it is not connected to the API, provider, booking, quotes or persistence and does not complete WP08. It introduces no authoritative road data, legal interpretation, geometry matching or pricing. The caller must supply directed crossing and applicability determinations for the relevant vehicle/product and time, and must assess source completeness, freshness and conflicts. Complete restriction evidence must cover the whole candidate route; an empty list alone does not establish coverage.

A confirmed prohibition applicable to a crossed directed edge is infeasible even when other evidence is stale or conflicting. Otherwise missing, stale or conflicting provider, graph or restriction evidence, missing revision identifiers, and unresolved relevant crossings produce uncertainty. Only complete consistent evidence with no unresolved relevant crossing produces feasible. The policy copies provider, graph, profile and restriction revision identifiers into every verdict. This classification does not certify safety or legal access. No evidence is fetched, stored or logged here; D27 access and retention controls remain unimplemented.

Current routing in `server/main.ts` uses OSRM alternatives and geometric checkpoint proximity, and can return the fastest route through a reported closure when no detour is found. This slice does not change that behavior.

Before activation: define and validate authoritative source provenance, review, confidence, directed edge matching, scope, validity and expiry; integrate provider/graph/restriction revisions and cache invalidation; handle outages and conflicts; implement immutable quote and atomic booking semantics; address privacy and retention; and design active-job re-routing and explicit exception policies. Acceptance requires independent review and operator sign-off, integration and concurrency tests, reverse-direction and stale/conflicting evidence tests, closure-during-job tests, immutable quote/replay tests, and at least 100 adjudicated routing cases per service area with measured ETA coverage. These gates are not claimed met by the unit tests in this slice.
