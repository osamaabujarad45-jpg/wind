# WP13 backend: balanced money ledger

Migration 16 (`server/db/postgres-ledger.sql`) adds PostgreSQL-only accounts, immutable journal headers and entries, cash receipts and append-only receipt events. Deferred constraint triggers require each journal to contain at least two entries with equal positive debit and credit totals at commit. Accounts, journals and entries reject updates and deletes. SQLite remains unchanged; all six ledger endpoints return `409 ledger_unavailable` there. The five tables are excluded from legacy snapshot import.

The import posts finished-order commission as debit driver receivable and credit platform revenue; discount funding debits promo expense and credits driver receivable. Confirmed remittances debit internal provider clearing and credit driver receivable. Adjustments debit or credit the driver receivable according to their sign, with the opposite entry in platform revenue. Pending and disputed remittances remain in `cash_entries` and do not affect the ledger. Business references identify the underlying facts, so repeat imports replay without duplicating journals; a changed payload fails `409 idempotency_conflict`. No transport revenue is rebooked. Rate snapshots are null where the actual historical rate cannot be established reliably; current settings are not substituted for historical facts.

Receipts snapshot the order price separately from the collected amount, collector and receipt reference. Assigned drivers and admins can record and collect; partial and full collections append events, reject over-collection and record the actor as collector. A return action records a returned event without rewriting the collected amount. The customer, assigned driver and admin may read; unrelated users are denied.

Projected exposure is the driver receivable balance plus active order commissions and the proposed commission. The configurable cap defaults to 100000 minor units and `cashCapActivated: false`. The guard requires the acceptance transaction and locks the driver user row when activated. It is not connected to acceptance while the finance-calibration owner gate is open; activation requires integration under the same transaction and independent finance sign-off of example journals. Provider clearing is an internal account only: WP14 bank settlement is not claimed.

## API contract

`POST /api/orders/:id/cash-receipt`, `GET /api/orders/:id/cash-receipt`, and `POST /api/orders/:id/cash-receipt/collect` (body `{amountMinor}` or `{action: "return"}`); `POST /api/admin/ledger/import`, `GET /api/admin/ledger`, and `GET /api/driver/ledger`. Anonymous requests return 401. Invalid amounts return `400 invalid_amount`; other participants receive `403 forbidden`; unavailable ledger returns 409.

## Verification

`node scripts/run-tests.mjs --filter=ledger-sqlite` passed: six unavailable gates, no ledger tables and legacy parcel/cash flow. `node scripts/check-persistence.mjs --filter=ledger` passed locally: migration 16, deferred balance and empty-journal constraints, immutable records, replay/conflict, idempotent import and driver reconciliation, receipt partial/full/over-collection/return and the disabled and injected enabled cap. `node scripts/check-contracts.mjs` passed (90 installed and 51 additive endpoint boundaries, 174 exchanges). `npx tsc --noEmit` and `npx expo lint` passed.

Backlog (independent DeepSeek V4.1 Flash review, confirmed by leader): append-only triggers do not block TRUNCATE by the schema owner (grant hardening belongs to the WP16 production-role cutover, not app migrations). Sign-aware negative-remittance import, order-insensitive journal replay, and zero-price receipt rejection were fixed after review with regression tests in `tests/ledger.mjs`. The shared `wp05_append_only` error text now reads `record is append-only` (fresh installs; already-migrated databases keep the old wording until their next rebuild or the WP16 cutover, and every assertion matches `/append-only/`, so the divergence is cosmetic).
