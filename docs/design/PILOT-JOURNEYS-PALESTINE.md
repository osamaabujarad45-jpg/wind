# Palestine pilot journeys

Personal Home starts with Ride, Send and Shop. A customer chooses one, then sees the relevant destination control. Booking, tracking, help and receipts keep the same vocabulary across the three services. Add more detail only at the decision that needs it. Use the existing Wind components and Arabic and English copy. [One-app flows](FLOWS-2026-11-26-one-app-profiles.md)

## Five riders in one vehicle

Today's booking accepts one to four riders. The car category has four passenger seats and the motorcycle category has one. Cargo vans have a separate cargo capability. Treat the example as one private group trip with a verified vehicle that has at least five passenger seats. Use the same passenger control and booking path when that option can be fulfilled. Show the price before requesting. A group of five can choose a split only by confirming two bookings separately, each with its own booker, fare, captain and trip code. Explain that choice if a larger vehicle cannot serve the trip. Use explicit consent for seat sharing and for any change to price or vehicle. [Current vehicle and seat rules](../../src/model.ts)

Before offering this corridor, validate the campus pickup and Sinjil drop-off as places with precise pins and locally understood landmarks, then verify the road and spare capacity with approved operators. Search by common Arabic and Latin spellings. Show checkpoint information with its update time. If route tiles or data are slow, show the text itinerary first and say when the route estimate is uncertain. Confirm the applicable vehicle and corridor permissions and insurance described in the pilot before accepting bookings. [Pilot commitments](../PILOT.md) · [OCHA road access reporting](https://www.ochaopt.org/content/humanitarian-situation-report-31-july-2026)

Birzeit University documented a student taxi reservation project in 2017. It supports testing a campus pickup. Validate current demand, local fare expectations and vehicle supply directly before publishing availability. Use Careem's vehicle categories, UberXL's private group size, HAAT's merchant delivery and Shiply's quote selection as interaction references. ## Profile switch and acceptance

Open the profile sheet by tapping the Account photo. Put Personal and each approved work profile in a short list. A pending Captain application opens its status. Captain availability and location end when the server confirms a switch away. A captain with an active pickup or parcel in custody resumes that job from the same sheet. Staff operate through authorized staff sessions. A switch changes the workspace while the server checks each action's role and revision. [Profile rules](FLOWS-2026-11-26-one-app-profiles.md#23-switch-rules-server-enforced)

Finish the Personal home and map performance slices, then build the session and profile switch slices in the existing order. Exercise the six journeys with Arabic and English landmarks, Arabic layout, large text and TalkBack on the physical A53. The five-rider release requires its route and approved vehicle checks, including a clear result when no vehicle can seat the party. Keep each service's proof and cash rules through a disconnect and retry. [Slice order and acceptance](FLOWS-2026-11-26-one-app-profiles.md#9-implementation-slices)
