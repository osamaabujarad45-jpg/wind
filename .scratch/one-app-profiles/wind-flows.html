Wind: one app, profiles you switch

A working sketch of the proposed flows. Tap your picture on the map, or the top card in Account, to switch between Personal, Captain and Shop, the way a Facebook profile switches to a Page. Pick a scenario on the right and follow its steps.

Sample people and prices. Nothing here talks to Wind's server. Captain mode is shown after dark, when the Automatic appearance turns Night for captains; it keeps whatever appearance the captain chose.

Switch rules the server enforces

  • Captain with a job in handCannot switch. "Finish your current job to switch", with Resume job.
  • Captain online, no jobOne confirm: go offline, then switch. Job alerts and location sharing stop.
  • Second phoneOne captain phone at a time. Signing in elsewhere opens Personal; to drive there, sign the other phone out with your password. A job in hand stays yours and resumes on the new phone.
  • Riding and drivingYou cannot go online while you have a ride as a rider now or in the next 2 hours. Handing in cash and finishing jobs always work.
  • Your own orderNobody is offered their own ride, and a shop owner never delivers their own shop's orders.
  • Customer on an active rideCannot go online as a captain until the ride ends.
  • ShopSwitch any time. With orders open, one Continue shows the count; the shop stays open and new orders show as a dot on your picture.
  • AdminNever a profile. Staff use separate, operator-enrolled accounts.
  • NotificationsCarry their profile. Tapping one from another mode switches, when the rules allow.
